Binding precedent and using older cases

Identify the court and the legal proposition necessary to its decision. The ratio may bind a lower court within the relevant hierarchy; persuasive observations and factual analogies play different roles. Later legislation and later appellate authority can change the applicable rule. For example, use older occupation cases with the current LRA framework, and distinguish the holding from an unqualified slogan.

Further directed Organisations research includes Tower Cabinet v Ingram, Re Smith & Fawcett, Magical Marking v Holly and Spectrum Plus. Read the assigned judgments and portal tasks before committing their detailed facts or reasoning to memory.

Explain it simply, then precisely

Priority is a queue with entry rules

Simple: several people claim rights over the same land; you need to know whose right survives the sale.

Where the analogy stops: the earliest right does not always win. Registration, value, occupation and overreaching can change the outcome.

Precise: classify the interest, identify the registered or unregistered regime, then apply its statutory priority and protection rules.

Two layers of co-ownership

Simple: the names on the legal title and the people entitled to the value are not always the same question.

Where the analogy stops: beneficial ownership is not necessarily a simple percentage contribution account.

Precise: distinguish legal joint ownership from equitable joint tenancy or tenancy in common; establish shares, severance and survivorship in sequence.

The company has its own legal identity

Simple: owning a company does not mean its bank account and debts are personally yours.

Where the analogy stops: guarantees, wrongdoing and statutory rules can still impose personal liability.

Precise: separate personality identifies the company as rights-holder and debtor; limited liability and the specific routes to personal liability require separate analysis.

Permission, process and duty are separate

Simple: having the power to do something does not mean you used the correct procedure or acted properly.

Where the analogy stops: outsider protection can preserve a transaction while the director remains accountable internally.

Precise: distinguish company capacity, agent authority, constitutional procedure, statutory approvals and directors’ duties.
