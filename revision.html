Quick concept guide — read, then close and test yourself
Fixed trust
The terms fix entitlements. Fixed does not mean equal shares.
Discretionary trust
Trustees choose distributions within a class. Membership does not itself give an equal share.
Bare trust
The beneficiary is absolutely entitled to income and capital; capacity affects when they can direct the trustee.
Interest in possession
A present enjoyment right, such as income as it arises, does not necessarily include the whole capital.
Vested / contingent
Distinguish an existing entitlement with enjoyment postponed from entitlement subject to a condition.
Power / duty
A power authorises an act; duties govern what must be considered or done. Check the trust instrument.
Care / loyalty
Care concerns the standard of administration. Loyalty concerns conflicts and unauthorised profits.
