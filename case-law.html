What stare decisis means here

Stare decisis means following binding legal precedent. Identify the relevant legal proposition, material facts, deciding court and later treatment before saying an authority binds the present court.

Ratio and obiter

The ratio is the legal reasoning necessary to the decision on the material facts. Obiter comments go beyond what was necessary and may be persuasive. A dissent is not the majority’s binding reasoning. A short memory cue is not a substitute for identifying the actual ratio.

Court hierarchy

For England and Wales, relevant Supreme Court and House of Lords authority binds lower courts. The Supreme Court can depart from its own and House of Lords decisions with caution. The civil Court of Appeal follows its own decisions subject to limited exceptions.

Use the right kind of treatment

Distinguish: a material difference means the earlier rule does not decide this case. Overrule: reject an earlier legal rule in another case. Reverse: change the decision on appeal in the same litigation. A newer decision does not automatically overrule everything older.

Privy Council and powerful obiter

The Privy Council is generally persuasive in England and Wales, subject to the specific express-direction mechanism in Willers. In Ivey, the discussion of the criminal dishonesty test was strictly obiter and was later adopted in Barton and Booth. Do not assume either that all Supreme Court comments bind, or that obiter must be ignored.

Read Willers v Joyce (No 2), paragraphs 4–12 and 19–21, including its discussion of Young v Bristol Aeroplane. The judicial Crown Court Compendium discusses the Ivey / Barton and Booth point. These precedent rules concern England and Wales; do not apply the hierarchy mechanically to another jurisdiction.
